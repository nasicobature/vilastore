Responsible Data Collection

Clear consent, protected contributor information, and restricted data access.

VilaStore follows responsible data-collection practices so each project can be evaluated, collected, reviewed, and delivered with appropriate privacy and data-protection safeguards.

Contributors participate voluntarily.
Contributors are clearly informed about the purpose of each project.
Consent is obtained before recording.
Contributors are informed that approved recordings may be used for AI training, testing, evaluation, or development depending on the client project.
Non-consenting people must not be recorded.
Contributor information is protected.
Access to collected data is restricted.
Data collection follows applicable privacy and data-protection requirements.
