Delete Your VilaStore Account

Use this page to request deletion of a VilaStore Marketplace account.

How To Request Deletion

Email privacy@vilastore.store from the email address on your VilaStore account with the subject line Delete my VilaStore account.

Include the account email address, phone number if one is attached, and whether the account is used as a buyer, shop owner, housing agent, rider, or delivery company.

What Will Be Deleted

  • Your sign-in account and profile information where deletion is allowed.
  • Optional marketplace profile content, uploaded images, and inactive user-generated listings where deletion is allowed.
  • Authentication tokens and non-essential account activity data.

What May Be Retained

Some records may be retained when needed for legal, safety, anti-fraud, accounting, dispute resolution, or transaction history purposes. This can include order records, delivery records, payment-related records, tax or business records, and messages needed to resolve active support or safety issues.

Timing

We will review deletion requests and respond to the account email. Deletion requests are normally processed within 30 days after account ownership is confirmed, unless a longer period is required by law or active platform obligations.
